Carolina Vázquez
Lead Designer & Art Director

I'm an Argentinian Designer and Art Director based in Amsterdam, specializing in visual identities and design systems. I studied Graphic Design at the Universidad de Buenos Aires (FADU), and built my career within advertising agencies, a path that shaped how I approach every project, both creatively and strategically.

Over the years I've worked on branding and campaigns for clients including Coca-Cola, Stella Artois, Foodora, Elf Cosmetics, Amazon, and Globant, with work recognized at Cannes Lions.

I'm currently a Lead Designer at &Walsh, where I execute as well as direct creative output across brand and campaign work, guiding projects from concept through execution while mentoring and leading the designers on my team.
